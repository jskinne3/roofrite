If you’re a Nebraska homeowner getting ready to replace your roof, you’ve probably spent a lot of time looking at different shingle styles and colors. But there is a hidden champion working beneath the surface that deserves just as much attention: your roofing underlayment.

Installed directly between your roof deck and your shingles, underlayment acts as your home's secondary line of defense against the elements. 

Traditionally, contractors used asphalt-saturated felt paper. However, modern technology has brought us synthetic roofing underlayment—and when it comes to surviving Nebraska's notorious weather, synthetic is easily the superior choice.

## Felt vs. Synthetic: What’s the Difference?

* **Traditional Felt:** This standby is made of paper or fiberglass mats that have been soaked in asphalt. While it’s initially the more budget-friendly option, felt underlayment is itself a heavy material, can degrade quickly when even briefly exposed to the elements, and tends to wrinkle and absorb dangerous moisture, leading to long-term degradation of your rafters and a breach of your home’s exterior envelope.
* **Synthetic Underlayment:** Engineered from tough plastic polymers like polypropylene or polyethylene, synthetic underlayment is a highly effective barrier between the elements and your rafters and roof deck. Brands like [GAF](https://www.gaf.com) have innovated new technologies in roofing materials, resulting in synthetic underlayment products like [FeltBuster®](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/products/feltbuster) and [Tiger Paw™](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/products/tiger-paw) which offer a highly durable and slip-resistant barrier that keeps the weather at bay.

## Why Synthetic Underlayment Wins in Nebraska

Nebraska homeowners know that our climate doesn't do "mild". From severe spring hailstorms and our sweltering summer humidity to autumn’s howling winds and the brutal blizzards of Nebraska winters, our roofs take a beating.

Here is why synthetic underlayment is the best choice for our extreme weather:

* **Superior Water and Wind-Driven Rain Protection:** Products like [Deck-Armor™](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/products/deck-armor) are designed to repel water aggressively, protecting your roof deck from severe Midwest thunderstorms and preventing moisture from getting trapped.
* **Tear-Resistance Against High Winds:** Nebraska plains see incredibly high winds. Synthetic underlayment won't easily rip or tear away from the roof deck if shingles are damaged during a severe storm.
* **Mold, Mildew, and Rot Resistance:** Because synthetic materials completely repel water rather than absorbing it, they are highly resistant to mold and mildew—a massive plus during our humid summer months.
* **Extreme UV and Weather Exposure Forgiveness:** If a storm damages your shingles or delays your roofing project, synthetic underlayment can remain safely exposed to UV light and severe weather for 90 to 180 days without degrading. Felt paper would rot and wrinkle in a fraction of that time.
* **Long-Lasting Lifespan:** Traditional felt only lasts about 12 to 20 years. Synthetic underlayment boasts a lifespan of 25 to 40 years, matching the longevity of premium modern shingles and securing your investment for the long haul.

## The Bottom Line

While synthetic underlayment comes with a slightly higher upfront material cost than old-school felt, its ability to withstand Nebraska's extreme seasonal shifts makes it a smarter investment. It guarantees a smoother finish for your shingles, reduces the risk of structural water damage, and ensures your home stays dry—no matter what the weather forecast throws your way.
